## What you will hear that is new

- Real names. "Dustin", "Alex", "Mike", "Alan", "Cade", "Evan", "Sholly" every day; "Dustin Kannedy", "Alex Kannedy", "Evan Kannedy", "Evan Sholly", "Mike Lababera", "Alan Meadows", "Cade Osterman" on the grid, in results, at the podium. "Kannedy" alone is never said.
- The podium, once the classification is in: *"Your podium: Verstappen wins it, Norris second, Leclerc third."*
- A winner with a penalty hanging over him is held, not crowned: *"...so we wait for the classification before we call the winner."* Then the classification settles it, either way, by name.
- Every penalty with its reason: *"Dustin is handed a five-second penalty after speeding in the pit lane."*
- The lobby: *"Good evening, and welcome along. Dustin, Mike and Alex already in the lobby with us."* Who we are waiting on; the ready count.
- Practice, called on cars (Day Programme §4): *"Welcome to practice. About thirty minutes on the clock... Nobody is racing anybody yet."* Then every league driver's personal best with its stakes — *"Mike improves. Nine tenths of a second found, a one thirty-six point zero, and he is seven tenths off the quickest time"* — the board as gaps, never positions, clock marks, the five-minute pivot, a summary with full names at the end. A history card still fills the quiet.
- Qualifying, called on cars (§5): flyers through sector one against his own best, results with provisional place, AI cars only when they take provisional pole, deleted laps with the clock as the stake, *"Last minute of qualifying. Whoever is on track now has one lap, and that is it."* Pole is held at zero until the Final Classification, then the grid: *"Pole position, Stroll, a one thirty-five point four. Our drivers line up like this: Alex fifth, Mike sixth, Dustin eighth..."*
- No "Oh, yes!" and no two lines opening the same way.
